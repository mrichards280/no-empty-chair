Good to know

The not-boring fine print

Full set vs. fill

New set from $85. Already have a base? Fills start at $55 every 2–3 weeks. We'll help you pick the right one when you book.

Length, shape & art

Short to XL, almond to coffin, a little art or a lot — length and design set the price, and it's all on the booking screen before you confirm.

Deposit & reschedule

A $20 deposit holds your seat and comes right off your total. Life happens — just give us 24 hours to move it.

Come bare (or don't)

Bare nails are perfect — or add a Soak-Off ($10) and we'll remove your last set. After: cuticle oil daily, gloves for dishes, and you're golden.

Ready to be obsessed?

Real-time availability. No deposit to browse times. Reschedule anytime up to 24 hrs.
