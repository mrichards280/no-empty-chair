Your base style adjusts for length (bra, waist, hip) and size (small, medium, jumbo). You'll see the exact total before you confirm — no chair surprises.

02

Hair's included

Premium braiding hair comes with every price. Prefer to bring your own? Take $15 off at checkout.

Arrive washed, blown out, and detangled — or add Wash & Prep ($35) and we'll handle it. Clean, stretched hair protects your scalp and your install.

A $40 deposit holds your seat and goes toward your total (48-hour reschedule). After: satin at night, oil the scalp weekly, book your takedown by week 8 — we'll remind you.
